## 📦 Requirements & Installation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This module automatically clears the stock Camera app data upon installation.**
> After flashing and rebooting, you will need to re-configure your in-app camera settings (watermark, grid lines, storage preferences, etc.).

### System Requirements
* **Device**: OnePlus 13 (OP13)
* **Supported ROMs**: 
  * ColorOS 17
  * Target tested firmware: `17.0.0.102`
* **Supported Root Managers**:
  * [Magisk](https://github.com/topjohnwu/Magisk) (v27.0+)
  * [KernelSU](https://github.com/tiann/KernelSU)
  * [KernelSU Next](https://github.com/rifsxd/KernelSU-Next)
  * [APatch](https://github.com/bmax121/APatch)
